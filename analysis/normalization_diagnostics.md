| India | pes / ssp585 | 5065 | 0.403 | 0.416 |
| India | **pooled** | 10130 | 0.378 | 0.383 |
- **Brazil** (Spearman rho=-0.445, n=10508): moderately offsetting — plants high on one hazard tend to be lower on the other.
- **Portugal** (Spearman rho=0.029, n=898): largely independent — being hit hard by one hazard says little about the other.
- **India** (Spearman rho=0.383, n=10130): weakly compounding — a mild tendency, not a strong one.
